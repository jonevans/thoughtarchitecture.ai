Midwest Metal Fabricators (MMF) is a privately held, mid-sized fabrication company specializing in precision metal components and assemblies for regional manufacturers. With three strategically located facilities across the Midwest, we provide the speed and flexibility of a local job shop with the capabilities and consistency of a larger operation.

We're known for being faster than the big shops and more capable than the small ones. Our customers stay with us because we're reliable, easy to work with, and we deliver quality parts on time.

Our Services

Core Fabrication Services

  • Laser cutting (steel, stainless, aluminum)
  • CNC forming & bending
  • MIG/TIG welding
  • Robotic welding
  • Secondary machining operations
  • Powder coating and finishing
  • Sub-assembly and integration

Engineering & Prototyping

  • Rapid prototyping for new product development
  • Design-for-manufacturability (DFM) consulting
  • Reverse engineering services
  • Short-run pilot production

Production Services

  • Small-to-medium batch production (10-2,500 units)
  • Annual blanket orders
  • JIT replenishment programs
